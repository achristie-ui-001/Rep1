# Megan's Review Book: Plan

Goal: a printed, hardcover book of Megan's TripAdvisor reviews, with her own
review photos, our phone photos, and a few web images, laid out by trip.

## Stages

1. **Harvest the reviews** (Megan's TripAdvisor data export, or saved pages).
   TripAdvisor blocks automated downloads, so this step needs you.
2. **Build the master list.** One record per review: place, city, country,
   date visited, date written, rating, title, full text, and her photos.
3. **Put them in order.** Default: chronological by trip, with each trip as a
   chapter (e.g. "Portugal, May 2019": the hotel, the restaurants, the sights).
   Reviews from home go in a "Close to Home" chapter.
4. **Add pictures.**
   - Her review photos (from the export).
   - Your phone photos, matched to each trip by the date and GPS location
     stored in each photo.
   - Web images only where there's a gap, using free-to-use sources
     (Wikimedia Commons, Unsplash) so the print shop won't reject the files.
5. **Lay out the book.** Cover, a contents page, a map of everywhere she's
   reviewed, a "by the numbers" page (countries, meals, average rating),
   chapter openers, then the review pages.
6. **Proof.** You get a PDF to read through. Fix typos and swap photos, then
   approve.
7. **Print.** Upload the print-ready PDF to Blurb (ships to Canada; takes a
   full-book PDF) or a similar service. Order one proof copy first.

## Folders

- `1-tripadvisor-export/`: the data export or saved review pages
- `2-phone-photos/`: phone photos, one subfolder per trip if possible
- `3-notes/`: anything extra (trip names, who came along, stories)
